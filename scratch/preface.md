The more what we once saw as *rottenness*, or even *evil*, begins to look like a deep form of confusion that we know intimately. That we can now help others escape.

Make no mistake: this is not a call to be *nice* or *gentle.* There really are profoundly dangerous people out there who will use those against us. But there *is* a way of being that enables us to fight their behavior without accidentally (though on another level, intentionally) reinforcing its deep causes.

I won't pretend to have uncovered the full depth and breadth of this way of being, much less actualized it in myself. But I've found a way of looking at it that reminds me it exists. That has helped me through countless challenging interactions that could have gone another, much worse, way.

I hope some of it is useful to you.
