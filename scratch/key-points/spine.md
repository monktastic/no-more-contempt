# The spine: the L0 theses, deduplicated and in book order

*Stateless. `index.md` holds every claim (T) and every way of saying one (F); this lists the T0 theses once each, in the order the book earns them, with the chapter that owns each. It's what a condensed book has to say and nothing it may leave out. As of September 10, index.md has 51 T0 and 54 T1 claims; the T0s reduce to the 38 below. The index keeps a thesis as separate bullets when each is a separate move in a chapter ("contempt is evil," "the cover is self-refuting," and "size doesn't matter" are three bullets there and one thesis here), and it keeps a thesis in two sections when it's seen from two sides (the handshake in §5 and §7). This list merges both, so it's the count and the index is the working file. The mapping: index T0s 2 and 7 are thesis 20 here; 4 and 5 are 22; 8 and 10 are 5; 9, 11, 13 are 24; 14, 15, 16 are 8; 20 and 22 are 6 and 9; 21, 30, 31, 32 are 19 and 33; 23, 25, 26 are 15 and 16; 27 and 29 are 26; 33 and 35 are 34 and 35; 38 and 40 are 25; 47, 48, 50, 51 are 36; 49 (being held while you look) is a framing of 36 and 37, not a thesis. The T1s are consequences and refinements; none adds a thesis that isn't here.*

## Is T0/T1 necessary and sufficient?

**Sufficient: yes.** Every row of `themes.md` and every sentence of the spine paragraph in `engine.md` is covered by a T0 or T1. Nothing at T2 or T3 is needed to get from the preface to the crescendo. (T2 holds the cultural marks, the psychology, the tools, and the Oneness elaborations. A condensed book can drop all of it.)

**Necessary: yes, as the 38 below.** Some T0s in `index.md` still overlap each other across sections (the shame paradox is in §1 as the layers and in §4 as the two collapses; the parasite's one trick is in §7 twice). That's tolerable in a working file. This list is the deduplicated count.

**Rating changes applied on September 10** (from L to T/F, with these moves): down to T1, "our culture fetishizes rationality," "how subtle contempt can be," "the shadow feeds on our pain," "the devastation we see is a mirror image," the Eisenstein paragraph; down to F, "it is a life-destroying force," "it is only by seeing how thoroughly we've been tricked," and about a hundred restatements now indented under the claim they restate; down to T2, "stay aligned with your heart"; up to F0, "the exception isn't what conscience registers, the not-looking is," "the hiding is the revealing," "why did you stop looking," "the essentialist feeling reads depth," "recursive mutual recognition," "shame is the parasite's last defense."

## The 38, in order

**Preface**
1. Something in you wants the worst people to hurt, beyond any good it would do. (pill test; §6)
2. "It's confined to my skull" is the lie that lets it out. (§5, §12 impact)

**Chapter 1**
3. Limbo: knowing and not letting yourself know, at once. (§1)
4. Harm requires turning away from the person, and turning away exists for harm or use: both directions, checked in your own case. (§1, §3)
5. Conscience is the part that keeps others in view; it never argues; it registers the exception, not the harm. (§2)
6. The hiding is the revealing: it has to be deniable and it must not be hidden; the provocation is aimed; nothing you do may be allowed to clear you. (§5)
7. It behaves like an agent and isn't one; borrowed equipment; not a literal thing. (§7)
8. Contempt is evil, as identification, not as a judgment; the cover ("mine is the good kind") is refuted by the sting; the smallest morsel carries the whole. (§3)
9. It teaches: the part of you that knew, and couldn't prove it, isn't worth listening to; that's how it spreads. (§5)
10. The paradox, seeded: something was using you, and you're fully responsible. (§1 layers)

**Interlude**
11. Three piles: what you saw, what I make of it, what I'm asking you to hold. (§14)

**Chapter 2**
12. The light: the part that cares, fierce, for all of it; the asymmetry (one direction is recognition, the other departure); love as orientation. (§12)
13. The darkness's message is not that good isn't real but that you get to decide what it means; its one goal is to make us forget there was a light to steer by. (§12)
14. Fierce is where the darkness gets in, and you can sense it from the sending end. (§4)

**Chapter 3**
15. We don't shame our enemies despite it making them dig in, but because of it; part of us needs them to be what we say. (§6)
16. Bad people are the collective dumping ground; the sacrifice; the most dangerous thing an enemy can do is get better. (§6, §8)

**Chapter 4**
17. The two collapses (villain / accident) and why the mind can't hold both; compassion holds both. (§4)
18. The darkness is real enough to sink a ship and made of nothing but a hole. (§7)
19. Our parasites collaborate: the handshake; the room; the feeling of standing outside the conspiracy fighting it is the conspiracy. (§7)

**Chapter 5**
20. The one choice: real at one point (keep looking or stop), no chooser after; the want is innocent, the label is borrowed; remorse's shape, not shame's. (§1)
21. Kind is not nice: the second stranger held both halves at no cost; niceness is the "couldn't help it" collapse, contempt the "he knew" collapse. (§4)
22. Two names for one state: "unconscious" for mine, "conscious" for yours; what limbo hides is the other's sameness. (§1)
23. Three distances: the man in front of you; nobody in front of you; the insect in the cave. (§1)

**Chapter 6**
24. You can't calculate your way to goodness; the devil can obey any rule; nothing in the book is a rule. (§2)
25. Two meanings of righteous; you can only choose the counterfeit by forgetting the real one; notice how sure you are. (§9)

**Chapter 7**
26. Once the verdict is in, the person looks like the verdict; when another's darkness looks intrinsic, your own has entered the seeing. (§7)
27. Nobody gets both views of one instance; conflation has two faces. (§7)

**Chapter 8**
28. Limbo is the water: most of what we do, most of the day, with no enemy in view. (§10)

**Chapter 9**
29. Why would I want to stop corruption when I feel so good judging it? Part of me doesn't; relieved or robbed. (§6)
30. Trying to be a good person is the trap; the idea needs bad people. (§2, §8)
31. The darkest idea: round up the bad people. (§8)

**Chapters 10 and 11**
32. Why contempt is the wrong that scales: no record, dressed as duty, public and rewarded, pleasant to watch. (§5)
33. A conspiracy without conspirators: it defends itself, recruits, adapts; two at war both grow; the Devil and Mara as this seen whole. (§7)

**Chapter 12**
34. Evil is the result of turning away, not its cause; the essentialist feeling reads depth; all evil is innocent, and everyone is fully responsible. (§8)
35. The far end: the knowing grows while access shrinks; the hostage at the window. (§8)

**Chapters 13 and 14**
36. Turning toward is the only stance that doesn't feed it; not-seeing isn't chosen, catching is trainable; compassion is not wisdom. (§13, §4)

**Chapter 16**
36a. Contempt for the world: the same turning away aimed at everything; the tree and the arch as the checkable seed; the rest is belief. (§11)
37. Compassion is the nature of mind; goodness is what remains when the hiding stops; the kind has one member. (§12)
38. Every act counts, witnessed or not: the sealed room; the stranger about to break; held like the parasite, no chooser and treat it as if there were. (§12 impact)
