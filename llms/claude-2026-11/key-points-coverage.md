# Key-points coverage of the four-chapter book

A snapshot of `short-book/chapter-1.md` to `chapter-4.md` as they stood on November 12, 2026. Points are keyed by their wording; the index line in parentheses is today's and will drift.

Read to each chapter's holding pen, and in Chapter 4 to the double bars. Material below the bars or in a pen counts as Missing. Grades follow the weight check: In is a scene, a phrase, an act or a check the reader runs; Thin is a label, a clause, an abstract statement or a generic case.

1. **Responsibility and choice.** 26, then 48 as its derivation, then 614. The chapter can open here because Chapter 3 already says "fully responsible" and Chapter 4 says "a turning from."

7. **Contempt for the still-contemptuous.** 458, then 565. The book's own reader is the likeliest to fall in here, so it closes the chapter.

0. **Opening check.** 519, the tree and the arch: the faculty fires where nothing has an inside. Mark it as the last thing the reader can run.

   1. 109, the culture's faith in rationality.

   5. 242, contempt survives as the last praised evil because it leaves no proof.
   6. 509, the cleverest minds build the best traps.
   7. Points back to 91, 301 and 511, all In.
2. **How that is the root of the biosphere's collapse.**
   1. 517, the world held as stuff.
   2. 523, people held as stuff follow from it.
   3. 501 and 508, the background hum and the atrocity it makes likelier.
   4. 521, we are the world, so we can't pull a fast one on it.
   5. 472, getting away with it, and who was watching.
   6. The Devil and the collaboration serve here: what is eating the world is one turning away with no planner. 350 and 369 are In; Chapter 6 restates them at the scale of the world. Points back to 504 and to 29's insect, both In.
3. **Why the way back can't be calculated.**
   1. 574, Darwin as the way we explain Love.
   2. Points back to 77, 91 and 577: reason in service of what knows.
   3. Points back to 617, held in Love, and 583, nothing is lost.
   4. The second half of 364 ends it: when you are actually fighting the darkness, it feels like love.
