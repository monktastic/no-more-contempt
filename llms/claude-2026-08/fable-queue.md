**Separate phenomenology from claims about his hidden mind, inside Chapter 1.**
The strongest objection in the review. The interlude now sorts this into "what
you saw" and "what I make of it", which handles it after the fact. The seam
inside Chapter 1 itself is still unmarked. Chapter 1 establishes what being
targeted feels like: aimed, tracked, deniable, self-confirming. It then treats
the target's reading of the other man's inner state as established. The fix is
not to weaken the chapter. It's to mark the seam where the account crosses from
what you felt to what must have been happening in him.

**Engage projective identification before making the originality claim.**
The interlude now has a plain-language "what people already know, and what I
think is new" passage that concedes the ground. `appendix-2` still claims the
literature stops before the two-person transaction, and that has to change.
Klein, Bion, Ogden, and the relational tradition after them describe: disown
something, attribute it to another, behave so as to induce the other to enact
it, then meet the result as confirmation. That is close enough that
`appendix-2` cannot say the literature stops before the two-person transaction.
What survives as Aditya's, and needs stating precisely: that the concealment
itself is the signal; that what the target reads is the other man's relation to
his own knowing rather than a projected affect; that deniability is structurally
required rather than incidental; that both parties' dark parts gain; and that
the lesson transmitted is distrust of conscience as such. Also flagged in the
review: Bateson, Girard, Braithwaite, Augustine and Arendt are each described
in a way that shrinks them. Fix that; the book's own ethic requires it.

**What "fully responsible" means, given that the turning isn't chosen.**
Chapter 4 asserts both and the review is right that the reconciliation is
currently rhetorical. The promising answer already in §12: responsibility lives
in how fast you catch it, which is trainable. But then it is graded by capacity
and cannot be declared full in every case. Needs deciding, not patching.
