# The short book's engine

*Spine agreed with Aditya on October 20, 2026. Not built or published. The long book has its own engine in `../engine.md`; this one is for the short book only, and when the two disagree about the short book, this one wins.*

## What the book is for

One recognition, which the reader should find they already had:

> My ordinary turning away takes part in the thing I most want to defeat, and remembering what I am is how I become able to help anyone else out of it.

The preface states it as what I saw. Chapter 1 reaches it at the scale of one encounter. The rest of the book takes it to its full depth and reach, and then says how to live it.

## The spine

Nine steps. Each is a recognition the reader checks in their own case, and each opens the question the next one answers.

| Step | The recognition | The question it opens | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **You knew.** Your own wrong was known and not let in, and the part that knew didn't argue. | What makes you hide it, and what lets you see it? | Ch1, Limbo |
| 2 | **Contempt makes you hide; the second stranger brings you back.** Fierce without the verdict works. | What were you and he both serving? | Ch1, The Two Strangers |
| 3 | **I'm the first stranger, and my fight feeds what I'm fighting.** My verdict makes more of his darkness, and part of me knew and wanted it. | What is it we're both turned away from? | Ch1, The Encounter to The Orientation |
| 4 | **What you are.** Harm needs a turn away; care is what's there when nothing is in the way; there is one kind of evil, the same turn at any size. | Then why does anyone turn? | Ch2 |
| 5 | **What turns.** Not me and not him: it hides from its host, shows itself to everyone else, and recruits through the license. The worst people are the same thing further along. | How much of my own life is it? | Ch3 |
| 6 | **More than I thought.** It's in the ordinary: contempt for the stupid, contempt consumed, the room, the water. (The quiet and cold kinds come earlier, in Chapter 1's Spread.) | How far does it reach? | Ch4 |
| 7 | **Everywhere, as one thing, and my fight is its work.** The handshake, the room, the institution, the world; the noble fighter is the conspiracy; every act of contempt feeds it. | Then how do I fight it at all? | The Picture I Use (5a) |
| 8 | **By remembering what I am.** The one stance that helps anyone out: catch it, relief or robbed, the healthy version, the other handshake. | — | Ch5 |
| 9 | **What I saw.** | — | Epilogue |

The book runs step 8 before step 7: the Way Through is Chapter 5 and the Picture follows it. A blind test on October 20 (`readers/ab-restructure-2026-10-20.md`) put the Picture before the Way Through and found it is where readers stop being pulled, in either position; placed first, it lost them before the practice. The far-end exercise and the Epilogue follow the Picture.

## Main line and the rest

Every passage is one of four kinds. The test for the first: would the reader still take the next step if this passage weren't here?

- **Main line.** The reader needs it to take the next step: a check, a recognition, or the question that hands off. It stays in its step's chapter.
- **Deepening.** True and worth keeping, but the step stands without it. It stays in its chapter for now.
- **Safety.** It protects a reader who'd be hurt without it: the abuse exceptions, the note for the hurt, the warning against self-contempt. It stays where it's needed, even off the line.
- **Off-line.** It answers someone who isn't on the page, such as the philosopher. An appendix or the long book may suit it better.

A passage can be split when a paragraph holds more than one kind. Before calling anything deepening or off-line, check it against `../../../scratch/key-points/index.md` and `../reader-lessons.md`: a top-tier point, any formulation under one, or a sentence readers vouched for is main line. The test above can't see that: the signal line, the remorse that runs one way, and the contempt for people I love all look like support for a step until you know what rests on them.
