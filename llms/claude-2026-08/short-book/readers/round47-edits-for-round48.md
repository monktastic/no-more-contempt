# The most recent round of edits

*These are the changes made to the book in its latest revision, every one of them in response to five readers who are all Mahamudra practitioners (Tibetan Buddhists of the Kagyu and Nyingma lines), as well as writers. The book you just read is the version after these edits. For each, "Before" is how the passage read until then and "After" is how it reads now.*

### 6. The introduction

**Before:**

> The main engine of this book is one familiar thing: *moral contempt*, the way we treat our enemies as though they were *inherently defective.* Traced carefully, I'll argue, it turns out to be a uniquely insidious expression of evil: how the darkness spreads between us while masquerading as *righteousness*.
>

>

I'll argue that you can't calculate your way to goodness, and say what's left when you can't.

> By the end I'll be claiming something stranger still: that this darkness doesn't only work through each of us but, in effect, across us, without being anything at all. An absence that behaves like a presence is hard to hold in mind. Every old picture of it grants it either too much substance or too little, and both mistakes feed it: the first breeds contempt, the second complicity. You don't need to believe any of that to read on.
