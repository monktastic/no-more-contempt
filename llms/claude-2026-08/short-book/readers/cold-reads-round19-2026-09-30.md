The chapter's "a change of heart" is the language of conversion. I could preach it, and it made the check work on me with James.

"None of this lets me off the hook, though. It's my mind, and I answer for what it does." Both versions have it, but the second file holds it back for five paragraphs, until after the part with goals has been built. The chapter puts it right after "it's not exactly "me" doing this." That's the right place. The escape hatch opens and you shut it in the same breath. In the second file I'd have spent five paragraphs thinking you were letting yourself off.

The hypocrisy passage is only in the chapter: "And hypocrisy only means something between two people who answer to the same thing." It was the best sentence in the chapter for me, and the second file doesn't have it. Whoever added it, keep it.

"He knows exactly what that is: harm that's sure it's good." (the chapter). The second file has, in its place, "—and that's a condition that part of me knows he will recognize as the hallmark of evil." The chapter's is a sentence a person says. The second file's is a sentence a paper says.

"The self-deception leaks out my eyes, and the harder I hide it, the more clearly he sees it." (the chapter) against "That self-deception leaks out my eyes, showing itself to my enemy, forcing me to hide it harder, which only helps him see it more clearly, etc., ultimately empowering it in me." (the second file). The chapter's is cleaner, and "etc., ultimately" is exactly where I'd stop listening in a sermon.

The chapter was also right to cut "That combination is both enough to produce the outcome I need, and also necessary." and "Here's another way to look at it." Both are lecture. And it was right to drop "The subtlest form of all is aimed at everything: a flatness toward the world itself, where trees and animals and nature itself are just so much inert stuff." Then comes "Calling it "contempt" sounds ridiculous, but it has the same essence, and originates from the same place." That's a metaphysics announced with no evidence. It's the one place in either version where I'd have put the book down and not picked it up. There might be a real thought in it for later, Calvin's theater of God's glory treated as furniture. It doesn't belong in a first chapter.

**What the second file has that the chapter should recover.**

Honestly, not much. Two things.

"And which side did you listen to?" (the second file, after "Why did one side have to argue, and the other not?"). It's a real question with no answer built in, and it turns the memory from something that happened to me into something I chose. For my reference call about H., the answer is that I listened to the side with the reasons. That put my responsibility on the table in the first section, before you start saying "part of me." I'd recover it.

"(which is something I *do* from limbo)" (the second file). The chapter's "So what exactly have I done to him? Nothing but show that I'm doing this to him." I had to read twice. The parenthetical says the step I was missing: the showing is itself done half-knowingly. I'm not sure it belongs verbatim, but the chapter's version needed that hinge, and this is it.

I'll mention one more without recommending it. The second file ends: "Part of me already knows that this is how it works. All that's left is to become fully aware of it, so that I can make a different choice." Part of me wanted a turn toward what to do, because a sermon needs one. But "All that's left" makes it sound easy, and it isn't. Fifteen years of spiritual direction say it isn't. The chapter's turn comes earlier and truer, in "to be seen as more than what I do." I'd leave this ending out.

**Order and length.**

The comparison confirmed the chapter's ending. The second file puts the loved ones in the middle of "Other kinds," then goes on to self-serving, then to a closing reflection. By the end, the sigh at my son had faded behind nursing homes and nature. The chapter saves the dinner table for last, and that's why it caught me. Keep it last.

The chapter's "Part of me knows it will spread, and that's part of why I send it." isn't in the second file. I only granted it tentatively, but it's truer of my clergy group texts than I'd like, and it belongs.

On length, the second file is longer, and nearly everything extra is the weakest material. So the chapter has already cut the right fifth. What's left to cut is what the two share and I flagged before: the invitation paragraph, the levels paragraph in Common knowledge, and the "Conscious but unaware" restatement. Both versions also make me wait too long for the cold version, and that's the one that has my name on it. I'd still move it up.

My refusal stands in both versions: "The only thing that benefits is *it*." The chapter adds "The relief I get is only the bait." That makes the agent more vivid, not less. If Chapter 2 is going to give this thing a name, I'd rather the first chapter say "acts as if" and stop there.
