The Cree have a word for the darkness: wetiko, the cannibal spirit, a hunger that eats life and is never full.[^wetiko] It is anti-life. Not a rival life with a plan of its own; it has no life of its own. It is life turned against itself, eating itself, and calling the meal good. Hunting for it in an enemy is one of the ways it hides. In those moments, look for the turn in your own eyes.

[^wetiko]: The word is theirs, and it carries more than I'm using it for. Jack Forbes, a Native American scholar, said wetiko was the disease the conquerors brought with them; he watched what it did to his people. Paul Levy, a writer who has spent his life on it, reads it as a mind-virus: it passes from person to person, hides inside its host, and gets its host to defend it. I know the word only through the two of them, and I borrow it because it names what this book's parasite does.
