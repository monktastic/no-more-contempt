# Best formulations

*A record, not a stateless document: the one deliberate exception, like the cold-read files. For each core claim, every wording that someone called the best yet, dated, verbatim, with where it lives and who judged it. Entries are added, never edited. The point is to see over time whether the book is climbing or walking an Escher staircase: whether a new "best" is better than the last one, or the same thing found again.*

*Who judged it matters. "Claude" means a model's opinion in a working session, which CLAUDE.md already says has been wrong. "Readers" means cold readers said so unprompted (their letters are in `short-book/readers/`). "Blind test" means the wordings were put side by side, unlabeled, before fresh readers. Only the last settles anything.*

*The test, when a claim has two or more entries: give three cold readers the competing wordings with no dates or sources, each in a few lines of context, and ask which made them see it and which they'd keep. Record the result under the claim.*
