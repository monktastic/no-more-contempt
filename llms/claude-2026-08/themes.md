# Themes: what each chapter does to each claim

*Stateless. One row per load-bearing claim, one column per chapter, and in each cell the grade the chapter leaves the claim at, with the section that does it. The grades are claim-triage's, plus two: **seed** (raised as a question or a shape, deliberately unearned) and **used** (taken as a premise, no new work). Read a row left to right and it should only go up. What's owed after a check goes to `todo.md`.*

## The two rules

**Too much.** The same grade twice in a row is an echo. Cut the later one unless it's a one-sentence callback. Chapter 5 is exempt once per row, by its contract ("everything the book claims, once"), but once means a sentence or a short paragraph, not a re-run of the device.

**Too little.** A cell that says "used" before the row has reached the grade the use needs ("as we've seen") is a fault in that chapter. So is a row that a later chapter depends on and that never got past seed.

Grades: seed < stated < demonstrated < argued < earned. "Used" sits at whatever the row had reached.
