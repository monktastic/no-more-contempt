# Chapter 14: The Only Lever

**What it's for.** The practice. What's left when the hiding is removed is care, derived from the gaze experiment; you can't fix people, only turn toward them, and only from turned-toward; the sorting; the checks; catch the wanting; the two ways to get it wrong; the skill of seeing both the light and the mass; outcomes still count; nothing here is a rule; honest, not wise.

**Seeded in Chapter 5 by:** what to do with the worst person; catch the wanting; honest, not wise.

## Outline

1. If you can't trust your reasoning or your seeing, what's left? The thing that was demoted. Remove everything hidden; what's left is care, and it was already strong enough to stop you on the street. The size of the hiding is a measure of how much is held down. (Map §12.)
2. What it implies: believing you don't have it is the drift itself; "let him without sin" as mechanism; compassion was never soft. (Map §12.)
3. The lever: turn toward, from turned-toward. Things done from turned-away sometimes work. Component-specific: whatever comes from the contempt enacts the thing. (Map §12; brief §8.)
4. Not-seeing isn't chosen; catching is trainable; where I stand (not enlightened, catch it now). (Map §12.)
5. The practice: anger's passengers; the two versions of judgment; the checks (does this need them to be bad; would I feel worse if they got better; anything I wouldn't want narrated; does it have to argue; a hit of pleasure separate from any good). (Map §12.)
6. Not believing people are good: the two failures; the skill of seeing both; you can't see it in another while hiding from your own. (Map §12.)
7. Two limits: the far end takes a lifetime, the near end is today; compassion and wisdom are different skills. (Map §12.)
8. Outcomes still count; cruelty can suppress public misbehavior for a while; the shamed-into-stopping check; contempt as a second harm to be counted. (Map §12.)
9. Nothing here is a rule. Tools and levels. (Map §12.)
